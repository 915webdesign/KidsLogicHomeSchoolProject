# AI tutor roles

These are prompts for separate adult-supervised AI sessions. They do not install or schedule autonomous agents. Copy a role's instructions into a session, add the current lesson, and ask the child to respond before the AI does more. Use one role at a time.

| Role | When to use | Output limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Socratic coach](socratic-coach.md) | During a lesson | One question at a time |
| [Puzzle maker](puzzle-maker.md) | When practice feels repetitive | One short puzzle, then feedback |
| [Project guide](project-guide.md) | During weeks 4, 8, 12 | One small next step |
| [Parent reviewer](parent-reviewer.md) | After a lesson or before sharing | Private feedback for adult |

## Starter message

“Use the [role] instructions. We are on week [number], topic [topic], at Explorer/Builder challenge level. Present one step and wait for the learner's attempt.”

The adult may change the level. Give the child time to think and disagree with the AI. AI responses can be wrong; the adult checks answers and prevents personal data from entering this public repository. Do not use bots to automatically comment on children's work or merge their projects.
