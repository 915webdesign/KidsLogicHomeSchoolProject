For grown-ups: how to play together

Use these games after the cup warm-up in Lesson 1. Let the learner answer first. Read aloud when helpful; take turns or replay for another learner. Explorer asks for one spoken or written reason. Builder adds an alternative or a checking question. A reason can also be a claim: Statement Sorter accepts either for the two supporting statements, then explains their role.

Dinner Countdown has no single correct choice. Ask whether the reason fits the chosen option. Completion marks show activities explored, not mastery. Everything stays in this page's memory and clears on reload. No accounts, tracking, network requests, or saved answers.
